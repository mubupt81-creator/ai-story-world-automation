Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AI Story World video publishing automation project handles information when its owner uses it to create and upload videos to the AI Story World YouTube channel.

Information and access

When the channel owner authorizes the application through Google, it receives the permissions needed to upload videos to that owner's YouTube channel. The project is not designed to access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or other Google services.

How information is used

The authorization token is used only to authenticate the channel owner's video-upload workflow. Video files, titles, descriptions, and related publishing information are used to prepare and publish videos to YouTube.

Storage and sharing

Authorization credentials are stored as private GitHub Actions repository secrets and used by the automated workflow. They should not be placed in source code or shared publicly. The project does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers. YouTube and Google process information under their own privacy policies when videos are uploaded.

Retention and revoking access

The channel owner can remove the stored GitHub secret and revoke the application's access from their Google Account security settings. Workflow logs and generated video artifacts may be retained by GitHub according to the repository and workflow settings.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials, including keeping OAuth secrets in GitHub Actions secrets rather than in public source files. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

This project is a video publishing tool and is not intended to collect personal information directly from children.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the AI Story World channel, contact the channel owner through the AI Story World YouTube channel.
